the problem

memory cards had turned into a commodity, with sandisk, samsung, kingston and a dozen white labels selling the same gigabytes at roughly the same price and every ad saying so in specs, which leaves the buyer choosing on price and nobody choosing sandisk.

what i did

i moved the brand off the spec sheet and onto the job the card actually does for a creator, which is letting them keep shooting without stopping to decide what to delete. that became one principle, autonomy, carried through two arcs, valentine’s for the moments you almost missed and the pursuit of photography, made with creators, in house and animated.

over the months the team went from four people to me, so the repeat work of variants and review loops moved into ai workflows and my time went to the taste.

why it worked

gigabytes get matched by the next launch, but a brand that stands for permission to keep going gives every future product a story to walk into.

why this is me

autonomy is the idea i keep selling because it is the one i keep chasing, and it runs all through my ladakh notes.
