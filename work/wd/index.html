the problem

gaming hardware ads all sound the same, dark and rgb and muscular, and none of them sound like the people buying, whose lives are full of family and festivals with gaming woven through.

what i did

i wrote the real world as if it were the game, so holi became a battle royale, world video game day became a quest you unlock, colleagues turned into characters and everyday frames got a hud. three reels for each arc, all written vertical first, and the mechanic was built to be reused for whatever cultural moment came next.

why it worked

a campaign spends attention once while a grammar keeps earning it, and the brand finally talked to the room the gamer sits in instead of the screen.

why this is me

i like building a rule once and letting it run, which is also why i write the prompt before i write the copy.
