the problem

eighty-odd premium villas on hosur road and every plot a different size, in an industry that sells off one show unit and one price sheet, so the instinct was to hide the variation. there was no marketing team yet. i was the first hire, at 23, reporting to a sales head with twenty years on me.

what i did

i turned the variation into the reason to buy. a family buying a premium plot is buying the right to shape a home, so different plots meant a different interior for every family, and every identical project nearby started to look like the compromise.

then i built the machine around that line, with objection counters and training for a sales team of ten to twenty, crm playbooks, media across the bangalore hni catchment and performance campaigns feeding a nurture engine. brokers move villas in bangalore, so they got their own ₹27 L evening for 350+ channel partners.

why it worked

brokers sell what they can say in one sentence, and when you hand them the sentence the room converts at more than twice the norm.

why this is me

i learned to build the machine before anyone asked me for the poster.
