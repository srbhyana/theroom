the problem

i wanted to prove product marketing without the title, so i took four live b2b companies, read each one from the outside and kept asking what the market was getting wrong about them, and who would sign a bigger cheque if they framed it right.

what i did

the method stayed the same each time. research first, with browseai pulling every competitor’s library, notebooklm reading it and three prompts per competitor for business model, content and observation, then the icp math cell by cell so the target came out of numbers instead of taste, and only then the line and the battlecard.

for spry that meant a proactive clinical operating system for independent clinics doing 15 to 35 visits a day, and my own note to them that the real opening was autonomy and the security that makes a clinic never switch. for flowace it meant we don’t track people, we track margins, with a saint voice and a devil voice for the two people who buy it.

why it worked

the frame decides who signs and how big the cheque is. writesonic framed as a tracker sells analyst seats and framed as an operating system it sells to the cmo, while zluri framed as three tools gets three small contracts and framed as one layer it gets the cio.

why this is me

once i have seen that a frame is wrong i can’t leave it alone.
