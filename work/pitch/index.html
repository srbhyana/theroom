the problem

pitchsense sells ai roleplay and enablement to sales teams, in a category full of practice bots all promising that reps will rehearse and improve, and saying it louder separates nobody.

what i did

i built the positioning from the field up, with a relevance matrix, a competitor matrix and an audit of what bots claim against what happens on real calls, then a narrative and a one-pager i rewrote until it argued for itself. alongside it went the parts sales could use the same week, forty sets of conversation starters backed by research, a broadcast system, the homepage brief and copy flow, and a field manual so the positioning outlives my time in the room.

why it worked

a tool that rehearses gets compared on price, while a layer that holds what the company knows about its buyers gets bought by whoever owns revenue.

why this is me

i redraft until the page argues for itself, and ten versions of one page is a normal week.
