how my mind works

I think like a mess. I ship like a system.

  1. captureeverything goes in raw, a competitor’s tagline, a stance from a meeting i wasn’t supposed to be in, one line buried in a 40-page brief
  2. let a shape formnot a plan, an architecture, because you have to throw enough at the wall before the wall starts talking back
  3. kill the sharp onessome ideas are good and still don’t belong, and with pain in heart, they go
  4. make a rhythmshort here, long there, breathe, hit, until someone else can follow it without seeing the mess
  5. ship the systemi think like a mess and i ship like a system, not despite the mess, because of it
